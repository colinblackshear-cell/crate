KALEIDOBOX · KB-1

How it fits your music

Make it at home. Play it out.

Kaleidobox is built for music that grows in layers: tribal drums under a drone, a vocoder line over a loop, a pattern that changes every eight bars. It works three ways.

In Logic

Every tool on its own track

Each instrument loads as its own plugin, with Rec and drag-to-Logic for MIDI and audio. Song building stays in your DAW.

On stage

The looper at the center

Bring the finished song back in, then record layers on the fly, cycle takes, throw effects and move between sections without stopping.

In your pocket

Catch the idea first

Hum it into Kaleidobox Pocket, chop it, put a beat under it and send the little song to your Mac when you get home.

The rack

Ten tools, one rail.

Every tool sits on the same track rail, follows the same clock and can feed any other: chords drive the vocoder, any sound can go to the looper, every sound has a pedalboard.

Drums

Analog-style drum machine with ritual and hand-drum kits.

Bass

Named bass sounds with keys, patterns and slow arps.

Synth

Two vintage-style synths plus slots for your own plugins.

Vocoder

Thirteen pedals, a mic locker of classic mic styles.

Sampler

Chop, spread to keys, play on 16 pads with note repeat.

Loops

16 layers, up to 60-minute takes, bar-quantized.

Line in

Guitar, keytar or voice through the full effects chain.

Drone

A tanpura-style drone box that never waits for Play.

Chords

A different chord on every key of your MIDI keyboard.

Effects

A pedalboard on every sound, plus show moves for drops.
