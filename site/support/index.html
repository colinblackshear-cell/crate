Questions

Mac

How do I install Kaleidobox on my Mac?

Download the installer from the Get page and double-click Install-Kaleidobox.pkg. The page walks through every step, with pictures of what the Mac shows. There is also a one-line Terminal install.

The Mac says the installer can't be opened. What is "Open Anyway"?

Kaleidobox isn't registered with Apple yet, so the Mac blocks the installer the first time. Click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once. If the button isn't there, double-click the installer again and go straight back; it stays for about an hour. Full steps are on the Get page.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS, plus Logic Pro or any AU host. The standalone app is included, so you can also use it on its own.

Using it in Logic

How do I use it in Logic?

Each tool loads as its own instrument plugin, one per tool, so every tool gets its own track. Make a Software Instrument track, click the instrument slot and pick AU Instruments > Colin Blackshear > the tool you want.

How do I get my mic or a guitar into it?

Use the Kaleidobox Input effect plugin. Put it on an audio track in the slot for audio effects, and the mic or line in goes through Kaleidobox.

I can't hear my mic.

The mic is off when Kaleidobox starts, so you don't get feedback. Turn on Monitor to hear it. If it still is silent, open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone, switch on Kaleidobox (or Logic), and pick your input in the audio settings.

Logic doesn't list Kaleidobox.

Quit Logic and open it again. Still missing? Open Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager, find Kaleidobox and click Reset & Rescan Selection.

Kaleidobox Pocket

When is Kaleidobox Pocket coming?

It is coming to TestFlight first, then the App Store. It isn't released yet, so there's nothing to download today. See the Pocket page.

How does Pocket get songs to my Mac?

With Send to Mac. Your iPhone and your Mac need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. See the privacy page for how it works.
