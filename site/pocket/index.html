Kaleidobox Pocket · iPhone

Catch the idea first.

Voice-memo simple. Groove-box deep.

Record, chop, add a beat, sing over it and send the little song to Kaleidobox on your Mac when you get home.

Six tools

Everything under your thumb.

Voice-memo simple, groove-box deep. Each tool has its own colour and lives one tap away.

Loops

Record a take, chop it into pieces and layer it.

Drums

A step grid for beats under whatever you recorded.

Pads

Play your chops from a grid of pads.

Synth

Play melodies and bass lines with your thumbs.

Chords

A different chord on every key.

Vocoder

Sing over the beat, headphones on, and turn your voice into an instrument.

The flow

From a hum to a song.

One REC button with a count-in, so you never miss the first beat. Wear headphones when you sing over the beat.

  1. RecordHit REC. A count-in, then you're on.
  2. ChopCut the take into pieces.
  3. PadsPlay the pieces on pads.
  4. BeatPut drums under it.
  5. Sing over itHeadphones on, vocoder on.
  6. Send to MacTo Kaleidobox, or into any DAW.
