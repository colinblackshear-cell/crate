Privacy

Privacy policy.

Nothing leaves your device.

Kaleidobox, Kaleidobox Pocket and this website.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers Kaleidobox for Mac, Kaleidobox Pocket for iPhone, and this website, kaleidobox.app.

The short version

Kaleidobox and Kaleidobox Pocket collect no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics or tracking, and no ads. Your audio and projects stay on your device.

The apps

  • No accounts, no tracking. You never sign in. The apps don't include analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and we don't receive anything about you or how you use them.
  • Audio stays with you. Recordings, samples, songs and projects are saved on your Mac or iPhone. We don't upload them anywhere.
  • Microphone. The microphone is used only to record, or to play through the effects when you turn it on. Your Mac or iPhone asks for permission first, and you can take it back in system settings at any time.
  • Send to Mac. In Kaleidobox Pocket, Send to Mac goes directly from your iPhone to Kaleidobox on your Mac over your own local Wi-Fi network. The Mac announces itself on the local network and the phone connects straight to it. It doesn't go through a server of ours or anyone else's. iOS asks for permission to use the local network the first time.
  • Sharing files. If you choose to share or export a file to another app or a DAW, the file goes only where you send it.

This website

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operations. We don't have access to personal data from them. The installer download comes from GitHub Releases, and GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that download.

Children

Because the apps collect no personal data, they collect none from children either.

Changes

If this changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to support@kaleidobox.app.
