Kaleidobox

A live music rig for your Mac: sampler, looper, drums, synths, vocoder and a song builder.

Thanks for trying it out! You need a Mac with macOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer. Intel and Apple silicon both work. It takes about a minute.

Easiest

Download the installer

Download Kaleidobox for Mac

  1. Double-click Install-Kaleidobox.pkg in your Downloads.
  2. The Mac says it can't check it. Click Done (or OK). This happens once, because Kaleidobox isn't registered with Apple.
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Install-Kaleidobox.pkg. Type your Mac password if it asks, then click Open Anyway again.
  4. The installer opens. Click Continue and Install, and type your Mac password.
  5. Open Kaleidobox from Applications or Launchpad.

On macOS 14 or older you can instead right-click (or Control-click) the installer, choose Open, then click Open. The installer puts the app in Applications, and the instrument for Logic (AU) and the VST3 in your Plug-Ins folder. To update later, download and run the new installer.

Or install with one line in Terminal

Comfortable with Terminal? This skips the security step entirely.

  1. Open Terminal: press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, press Return.
  2. Copy this line, paste it into Terminal (⌘ V) and press Return:
    /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://github.com/colinblackshear-cell/crate/releases/latest/download/install.sh)"
  3. When it says Done!, open Kaleidobox from Applications.

Use it in Logic

  1. Quit and reopen Logic if it was open while you installed.
  2. Make a Software Instrument track.
  3. Click the instrument slot and pick AU Instruments > Colin Blackshear > Kaleidobox.

Other music apps (Ableton Live, Reaper, Bitwig) can use the VST3 version.

If something goes wrong

Logic doesn't list Kaleidobox

Open Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager, find Kaleidobox, and click Reset & Rescan Selection. If that doesn't help, restart the Mac and open Logic again.

It can't hear my mic

Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and switch on Kaleidobox (or Logic). Then pick your input in Kaleidobox's audio settings.

"Kaleidobox is damaged and can't be opened"

That's the same warning in a scarier form. Run the installer again, or use the one-line Terminal install; both fix it.
