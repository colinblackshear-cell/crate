The Kaleidobox Field Guide

Installation edition · For Macs on this planet and nearby ones

Kaleidobox

Getting the box onto your Mac

About a minute. Nothing breaks.

Kaleidobox is a live music rig: drums, bass, synths, a vocoder, a looper, a sampler and a song builder in one window. It runs on its own and inside Logic. You need a Mac with macOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer; Intel and Apple silicon both work.

The install

Five steps. Start at the top.
01
Entry 01

Get the installer

One file. It lands in your Downloads.

Download Kaleidobox for Mac

Then double-click Install-Kaleidobox.pkg in your Downloads folder.

02
Entry 02

The Mac gets nervous

Totally normal. Click Done.

Kaleidobox is made by a friend, not bought from a store, so it isn't registered with Apple. The first time, the Mac stops and says so:

"Install-Kaleidobox.pkg" Not Opened
Apple could not verify "Install-Kaleidobox.pkg" is free of malware…
Move to TrashDone

Click Done (on older Macs it says OK). Don't move it to the Trash.

03
Entry 03

Open Anyway

Tell the Mac it's a friend. Only once.

Open System Settings (the gear in the Dock, or the Apple menu > System Settings), click Privacy & Security, and scroll down to Security:

"Install-Kaleidobox.pkg" was blocked to protect your Mac.
Open Anyway

Click Open Anyway, type your Mac password if it asks, then click Open Anyway once more.

On macOS 14 or older there's a shortcut: right-click (or Control-click) the installer, choose Open, then Open.

04
Entry 04

Install

Continue, Install, password. Done.

The Kaleidobox installer opens. Click Continue, then Install, and type your Mac password. It puts the app in Applications, and the instrument for Logic and the VST3 in your Plug-Ins folder. Quit Logic first if it's open.

05
Entry 05

Make some noise

Open it. Press anything.

Open Kaleidobox from Applications or Launchpad. The first time it uses your mic, the Mac asks: click Allow. The ? button and the Guide inside the app show you around.

In Logic: make a Software Instrument track, click the instrument slot and pick AU Instruments > Colin Blackshear > Kaleidobox. Ableton Live, Reaper and Bitwig can use the VST3.

The shortcut

For people who like Terminal.
⌘
Optional

One line, no warnings

Skips steps 02 and 03 entirely.

Open Terminal (⌘ Space, type Terminal, Return), paste this line and press Return:

/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://github.com/colinblackshear-cell/crate/releases/latest/download/install.sh)"

It installs the same three things. Run it again any time to update.

If the universe misbehaves

Logic doesn't list Kaleidobox

Quit Logic and open it again. Still missing? Open Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager, find Kaleidobox, and click Reset & Rescan Selection. If that doesn't do it, restart the Mac.

It can't hear my mic

Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and switch on Kaleidobox (or Logic). Then pick your input in Kaleidobox's audio settings.

There's no Open Anyway button

It only appears for about an hour after you try to open the installer. Double-click Install-Kaleidobox.pkg again, click Done, and go straight back to Privacy & Security.

"Kaleidobox is damaged and can't be opened"

That's the same warning in a scarier form. Run the installer again, or use the one line in Terminal; both fix it.
