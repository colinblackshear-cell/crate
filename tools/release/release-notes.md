**Kaleidobox {{VERSION}}** for Mac (macOS 11 or newer, Intel or Apple silicon).

### Install

Download **Install-Kaleidobox.pkg** below and double-click it. The first time, the Mac says it
can't check it: click Done, then open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click
**Open Anyway**. The installer puts the app in Applications and the Logic (AU) and VST3 plugins in
your Plug-Ins folder.

Comfortable with Terminal? This one line installs everything with no security step:

```
/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://github.com/colinblackshear-cell/crate/releases/latest/download/install.sh)"
```

Step by step: https://colinblackshear-cell.github.io/crate/get/

### In Logic

Software Instrument track > AU Instruments > Colin Blackshear > **Kaleidobox**.
