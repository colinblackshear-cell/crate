The Kaleidobox Field Guide

Kaleidobox

A live music rig: drums, bass, synths, a vocoder, a looper, a sampler and a song builder in one window. This puts three things on your Mac:

  • The appKaleidobox, in Applications
  • For Logicthe Kaleidobox instrument (Audio Unit)
  • VST3for Ableton Live, Reaper, Bitwig and friends

Quit Logic if it's open, then click Continue and Install. The Mac asks for your password.

About a minute. Nothing breaks.
