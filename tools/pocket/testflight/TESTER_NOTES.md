## Beta App Description

Kaleidobox Pocket is a sketchpad for music ideas. Hum or sing into it, tap out a beat, add a bass
line or a synth, and stack it all up as loops. Bend any sound with effects, chop a recording into
pads, sing through the vocoder, then send the idea to Kaleidobox on a Mac or export it for a DAW.
You don't need to be a musician to have fun with it.

## What to Test

Thanks for trying Pocket! Headphones help when you sing over a loop.

- Record something (your voice, a sound in the room) and add it as a layer.
- Add drums, bass or synth, and tap TO LOOPER to stack them.
- Try the effects and the pads.
